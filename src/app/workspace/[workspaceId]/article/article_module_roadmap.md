# Article Module: Advanced Feature Roadmap

This roadmap outlines high-impact, advanced features designed to transform the Article module from a content editor into a comprehensive, data-driven content engine.

## 1. Intelligence & Automation Phase
Features focused on leveraging AI and data to optimize content before it goes live.

### [AI] Content Repurposing Engine (IMPLEMENTED)
- **Article-to-Social**: One-click transformation of a long-form article into a Twitter thread, a LinkedIn summary, or a concise Instagram caption.
- **Smart Summarization**: Automatic generation of TL;DRs and meta descriptions.
- **Tone Transformer**: Real-time adjustment of content tone (e.g., Professional vs. Casual vs. Humorous).

### [SEO] Semantic Optimization (IMPLEMENTED)
- **Real-time Scoring**: A sidebar that scores your content based on target keywords and readability.
- **Linking Suggestions**: AI suggestions for internal links to other workspace articles and external authoritative sources.

---

## 2. Workspace Collaboration Phase (NEXT)
Transitioning from a single-user tool to an enterprise-grade collaborative suite.

### [Live] Real-Time Collaborative Editing
- **Multi-cursor Support**: See where teammates are editing in real-time (Yjs/WebRTC integration).
- **Inline Comments**: Highlight text to leave feedback, tag users, and resolve discussions within the editor.

### [Flow] Advanced Approval Workflow
- **Roles & Permissions**: Multi-stage approvals (e.g., Writer -> Editor -> Legal -> Published).
- **Workflow Automation**: Automatically notify reviewers via email or system pulse when a draft is "Ready for Review".

---

## 3. Analytics & Performance Phase
Closing the loop between publishing and performance data.

### [Stats] Unified Multi-Platform Analytics
- **Aggregation Dashboard**: View likes, shares, comments, and impressions from all connected platforms in a single, unified view.
- **Post-Level Insights**: Drill down into specific articles to see which platform performed best.
- **Smart Timing**: AI-powered suggestions for the "Best Time to Post" based on historical engagement data.

### [Growth] A/B Testing Engine
- **Headline Testing**: Support for creating two versions of a post with different headlines to test performance.
- **Dynamic Content**: Serve different variations of content based on the target platform's audience data.

---

## 4. Content Integrity Phase
Ensuring reliability and long-term content management.

### [History] Version Control & Timelines
- **Snapshot History**: View a complete timeline of changes with the ability to "Time Travel" and revert to any past version.
- **Visual Diff**: A clear comparison view showing what text was added, removed, or modified.

### [Asset] Advanced Media Management
- **In-Editor Image Editor (FOUNDATION LAID)**: Basic cropping, filtering, and annotation directly within the application.
- **External DAM Integration**: Connect directly to tools like Canva, Unsplash, or Pexels for seamless asset sourcing.
